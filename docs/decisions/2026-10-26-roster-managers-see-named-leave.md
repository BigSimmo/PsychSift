# Decision: Roster managers see their team's planned leave by name

- **Status:** decided 2026-10-26
- **Source:** `docs/privacy-impact-assessment.md`, find "reconfirmed by the owner on 2026-10-26"

On 26 Oct 2026 the owner chose "Yes, managers see names" on a decision card in the privacy database
thread (privacy audit DB-09), confirming the named manager leave view first approved on 27 Sep 2026.
A roster manager of a confirmed team sees each active member's name, leave kind (annual, PD, exam
or personal), dates and status for leave the doctor attached to that team, to plan cover. Ordinary
members still see only an anonymous count, and no leave reason is stored. This does not replace the
other conditions before real staff use: the health service's privacy approval, the staging isolation
proof and the app-server location decision.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
