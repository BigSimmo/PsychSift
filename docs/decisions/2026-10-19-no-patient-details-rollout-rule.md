# Decision: No patient details is the firm rule for any rollout while privacy items are open

- **Status:** decided 2026-10-19
- **Source:** the owner, in chat

Repository audit F-38 found 4 of 11 privacy release items open (OpenAI zero data retention, the
APP 8 overseas disclosure notice, the APP 1 and 5 privacy notice, and partial patient-detail
minimisation). The owner accepted the recommendation that any rollout keeps the rule that no
patient details are entered, while the owner asks OpenAI about zero data retention and a privacy
adviser reviews the two notices.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
