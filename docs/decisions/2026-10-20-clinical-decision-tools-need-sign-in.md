# Decision: Answer and the patient-tailored tools need sign-in before 1 November 2026

- **Status:** decided 2026-10-20
- **Source:** `docs/clinical-hazard-analysis.md`, find "the owner reversed that because of the amended exemption in force from 1 November 2026"
- **Retires:** `answer-open-to-signed-out-visitors`

The regulatory audit of 20 Oct 2026 found that the TGA's amended clinical decision support exemption,
in force from 1 November 2026, does not cover decision support given to people who are not health
professionals. The owner accepted its recommendation ("Go ahead with all remaining recommendations")
to require sign-in for Answer, the medication patient details panel and its interaction flags,
therapy ranking, questionnaire scoring, and opening original documents. This replaces the
19 October decision that Answer stays open to signed-out visitors. The screens are built only after
the owner approves the before-and-after mockup.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
