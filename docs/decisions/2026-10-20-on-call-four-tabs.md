# Decision: On Call has four places: Now, Call, Shift and Handbook

- **Status:** decided 2026-10-20
- **Source:** `src/lib/work-frame/areas.ts`, find "Four places, one job each"

On 20 Oct 2026 the owner approved the On Call overhaul mockup and asked for it to be built. On Call
now has four tabs, each with one job. Now answers who to ring this hour. Call holds every number, the
referral routes and who is on. Shift holds the handover, the call log and breaks, joined by a switch.
Handbook holds how the hospital works: the playbook, how to refer, starting here, who's who and the
pocket card. Pages that list or edit a person's own entries keep their addresses and are reached from
inside these tabs, so no stored entry is hidden.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
