# Decision: answer-feedback notes are deleted after 30 days

- **Status:** decided 2026-10-26
- **Source:** `docs/privacy-impact-assessment.md`, find "The owner approved the 30-day note purge on 2026-10-26"

On 26 Oct 2026 the owner accepted the recommendation, in the privacy page thread, that the
optional note on a "Report a problem" answer report is deleted 30 days after it is written. The
content-free report category is kept. The purge job was already on `main` from 20 Oct 2026; this
records the owner's approval of it (privacy audit finding SB-06).

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
