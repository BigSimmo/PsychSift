# Decision: Work mode layout fixes from the 26 Oct audit

- **Status:** decided 2026-10-27
- **Source:** the owner, in chat

On 26 Oct 2026 the owner asked for a full audit of every Work mode page, taking the On Call
improvement (un-bunched phone numbers) as the standard of fix rather than its look. The audit
mocked up nine fixes from real screenshots. On 27 Oct 2026 the owner said "Go ahead with all
recommendations" and "Build".

- Crisis line rows (Admin Numbers, and the crisis card under clinical answers, which shares them)
  put the name first, the number on its own line, then the hours. No wording changes.
- Teaching Progress shows the four kinds of experience two per row on a phone, so no label breaks
  mid-word.
- Admin Today's renewal bar drops the middle "Today" label once the date has passed.
- Admin Requests cards say what was asked and when in one plain line, keep Chase as a tag, and
  show Copy again, Edit and Remove as quiet text buttons.
- New job lists its paperwork one item per line, and job contact numbers stand out like On Call's.
- My Day's month calendar on a computer lets each entry run to two lines.
- The Assessments ring reads "of 47" so it fits inside its circle.
- The Assessments glossary puts every term above its meaning.
- CPD Learning states its checked date once, and every "Demo mode is read-only" or "Demo data"
  note now begins "These are example records".

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
