- **Status:** decided 2026-10-26
- **Source:** the owner, in chat

On 26 Oct 2026 the owner reviewed the first round of accessibility mockups and said "lots of these make the design look worse". After a gentler second round, the owner said "Ok go ahead I approve gentle version but the actual main default doesn't change".

- Form and search box edges keep their current colour.
- The first-round proposals (darker box edges, rebuilt quick-action row, near-white tag, single-key shortcut switch, forced-colours Teaching action) are not built.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
