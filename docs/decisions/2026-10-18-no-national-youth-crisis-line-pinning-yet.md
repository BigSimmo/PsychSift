# Decision: National youth crisis lines are not pinned for CAMHS crisis searches until the owner approves the list

- **Status:** decided 2026-10-18
- **Source:** `docs/outstanding-issues-inbox/dd1b66e0-1761-474c-b9bd-280288c5e294.json`, find "Owner ruling 18 Oct 2026 00:10 AWST: do NOT pin national youth crisis lines"

On 18 Oct 2026 at 00:10 AWST the owner ruled that national youth crisis lines (Lifeline WA, Suicide Call
Back Service, Kids Helpline, 13YARN) are not pinned for CAMHS crisis searches until the owner approves the
exact list, and any future pinning must skip closed, planned, superseded or temporarily unavailable services
and keep CAMHS Crisis Connect and MHERL first. No routing changed: whether the ruling also covers Lifeline WA
on the existing `adult_metro_crisis` route (third for "youth self harm") is an open owner question on #8CKX6F.

If this summary and the source ever differ, the source wins.
